Location & Climate

Atacama stretches about 1,000 km along the Pacific coast of northern Chile, between the Andes mountains in the east and the Chilean Coast Range in the west. Together they block almost all moisture, which is why it is so dry. It covers roughly 100,000 km² of plateau at about 2,000–4,000 m.
